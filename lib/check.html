Renderer check

Draws an initial for every kind the renderer has (every pattern, texture, frame, band, corner, leaf, flower and the other choices), the built-in styles, the kept initials and some fresh ones, and compares each with the baseline kept in this browser. Keep a baseline before you change the renderer; after, open this page again and it shows anything that came out different: the baseline on the left, now on the right. Same machine and browser for both: other GPUs differ by a pixel here and there.
